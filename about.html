How Shopzzy Began

Shopzzy started in a compact design studio on 100 Feet Road, Indiranagar. We noticed that fashion retail in India was split between low-durability fast garments and overly formal occasion wear. Daily apparel that felt comfortable in a Bangalore cab, an office meeting, or a weekend coffee spot was surprisingly difficult to find.

We set out to create apparel that lasts multiple years, not just a few washes. We choose heavy cotton piqué for our collars so they never curl, double-stitch inner seams on our trousers, and test our dyes against hard city tap water.

Fashion for Every Age Group

Most fashion houses cater exclusively to 20-somethings or separate kids and parents into disconnected sections. At Shopzzy, our pattern makers share the same studio floor. The cotton knit used for our men's polo shares the same yarn supplier as our toddler crewneck sweaters and women's relaxed tunics.

We believe good clothes give you confidence quietly. You don't need giant brand logos across your chest to look put-together. Clean lines, honest tailoring, and durable cloth do the work for you.
